## Limitations

* Kernels: the rivals run FlashAttention-2's forward/backward per tile; Stream-CQSA runs its own CQS-masked kernels (classic CUDA / wave / Triton as routed) and pays the quorum-set redundancy; FA-2 resident is the plain kernel. Precision is fp16 with fp32 accumulation for all.
* A capped A100 80GB PCIe models a smaller capacity, not a smaller or slower device; nothing is claimed about edge hardware.
* GQA and other head layouts were not run (unsupported by the shape used).
* Full-model comparators (MEMO, FPDT, FlexGen) are out of scope; single-token decode is a different workload and is not included.
